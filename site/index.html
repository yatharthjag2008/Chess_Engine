      About

      A neural network (a small ResNet conditioned on rating and clocks) learned from real Lichess games which move a player of a given rating makes. It predicts the human move 41.7% of the time on games it never saw, more often than Stockfish's best move (37.6%).

      Game review, hints, the coach, adaptive difficulty and styles need Stockfish and the Python server: run the full app on your computer.
